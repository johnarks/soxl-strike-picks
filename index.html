Method

Each weekday at ~10:05 AM ET, after the open: take SOXL's live price, compute the standard deviation of its trailing 13 weekly returns, and set the call strike at price × (1 + k·σ) and the put at price × (1 − k·σ), rounded to whole dollars. k = 1.0 normally, widened to 1.25 when there's been a >25% weekly move in the last month or the day's news sentiment on SOXL is extreme (≤3 or ≥8 out of 10, from the leveraged-ETF news pipeline). The "inside rate" shown is the share of the last 52 weeks that stayed within the band — the historical odds of both legs expiring worthless.

Settlement: on the 7th calendar day, compare that day's close (or the prior trading day's if the market is closed) against the strikes. No option-chain data is used — this tracks whether the price stayed inside the strikes, which is what decides whether a short OTM call/put expires worthless.
